- **Today's 4 ships keep their stats exactly.** Only their pictures and model names change.
- **Sub hunting:** the Corvette does 2.2× damage against submarines and the Destroyer 1.6×, using the per-unit bonus the game already has. The Attack Sub and the patrol plane stay the best sub killers.
- **Research:**
  - The Corvette is known from day 1.
  - The Destroyer comes from about day 7, with Naval Gunnery, ASW Torpedoes and Area Missile Defence.
  - The Ballistic Missile Sub comes from about day 18, with Quiet Patrol and Extra Missile Tubes.
- **The computer:**
  - It builds Corvettes against enemy subs and convoy raids, counting them as half a ship so they don't crowd out bigger ships.
  - Destroyers go into escorts, raids and patrols. It keeps building Frigates.
  - At most 1–2 missile subs a nation, mainly for nations far from their enemies.

**The Ballistic Missile Submarine (your answer to plan 2 question 5)**
- **Missiles:** it carries 4 (6 with research): cruise, ballistic, Nuclear Cruise and Nuclear Ballistic. No chemical missiles and no ICBM, and at most 1 of each nuclear kind per sub.
- **Loading:** only in port, at your own Naval Yard. Missiles on board count inside the nation's limits, so there are still at most 5 nukes in all.
- **Firing:** it fires when stopped at sea, with plan 2's ranges measured from the sub (cruise 2,880 km, ballistic 4,680 km). Interception works exactly as for missiles from land.
- **Hidden:** it stays hidden like the Attack Sub. Firing shows it to everyone for 12 hours (6 with Quiet Patrol, conventional missiles only).
- **If it sinks:** its missiles are lost, and its nukes don't explode.
- **One honest point:** it gives every nation with a Naval Yard V a ballistic reach from any coast. Sea nukes could come about day 35–37, before the ICBM (about day 44). My suggestion is to keep plan 2 as it is, with the 12-hour reveal as the brake (question 7).
