**The Logistics Depot**
- **Where:** any province you own, city or countryside. One per province, no levels. Islands with no land link can't have one, since it would do nothing.
- **Price:** $50M, 10k materials and 5k iron. It takes 2 days and has no upkeep, like a Bunker level 1. For infantry and tanks it's worth about three road upgrades, and it's the best deal in mountains.
- **Limit:** 1 depot for every 5 provinces you own, at least 2. That's Germany 5, Russia 58, Israel 2.

**Your +25%, and how it stacks**
- **Half-trip rule:** a trip between two provinces is half in each. With a depot at both ends the trip is 25% faster; with one at one end only, about 11% faster. Moving inside a depot province is 25% faster.
- **It multiplies with everything:** roads and highways, terrain, weather, research and commanders. Infantry on a highway between two depots moves 1.30 × 1.25 = 1.63 × normal.
- **Stack-size slowdown:** a depot doesn't cancel it. A 20-unit stack gets 0.896 × 1.25 = 1.12 × normal.
- **Rail stays fastest:** trains get no depot bonus, and road plus depot is capped just under rail. Only wheeled units on highways hit that cap; they get about +14%.
- **Who benefits:** you and your allies only, like railways. If an enemy takes the province, the depot becomes theirs. A depot hit by a strike gives no bonus until it's repaired.

**What it does in practice:** a day-long march gets 2–3 hours shorter, and a tank column of 3 goes from 15 to 12 hours. It mostly helps you defend and gather forces behind your border.

**The look (sheets):**
- In a depot province, roads and highways are drawn about 1.8 times thicker with a dark edge, from the centre to the border. They stop at the border; you can see the Cottbus road ending at the Polish border.
- They read clearly at play zoom by day and night, on phone and desktop. In a city, the main roads through town get thicker, not the town's own streets.
- There's no model, so your province-building art rule holds.
- An enemy's depot only shows where you can see that province.

**The computer:**
- It builds one at a time, on routes from its capital to its borders, working back from the border, mostly in the countryside. Borders facing an enemy come first, and at war its attack front comes first.
- About 3 plus a third of its border provinces: Russia up to 16, the USA 13, Germany 5.
- Only when it has cash to spare, and never while saving for a road or railway.

**One thing I found:** today a road trip loses the leftover part of its last hour. Only trains and ships carry it into the next trip. So a +25% bonus shows up unevenly: 1 hour gained here, 3 there. Carrying the leftover for roads too would make the bonus real every time, but it would also make all marching a little faster, about 5–10% on average (question 1).
