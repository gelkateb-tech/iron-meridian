# Releases: dev build → public build

How it works from now on:
- Every finished change goes to the **Dev Build** first: https://claude.ai/artifact/EKDR7pckn3hPkoJVuxoe7R
- Each change is noted below under **Waiting for public**, newest last.
- The **public build** (https://claude.ai/artifact/PegnoUY4juYxMxy64YDTS4) is only updated when the owner says to move changes to public. Then everything under "Waiting for public" is published, moved to **Released**, and tagged in git.

## Currently public
- v3.12 (commit 5b47dd8): everything in v3.11, plus the battery saver (60 fps while moving the map), two research slots, sharper cities, mobilization rules, no city markers, the rules split, the new Higgsfield pictures and the short briefing.

## Waiting for public
- Strike and blast effects drawn in code (no video, light on phones), one look per weapon: cruise missile (quick orange fireball, sparks, small dark puff), ballistic missile (white flash, ground shock ring, big fireball, thick smoke column), chemical (small burst, then a yellow-green gas cloud that spreads slowly and lingers), tactical nuclear (blinding flash, fireball, fast shock ring, small mushroom cloud) and ICBM (screen-wide flash, huge fireball, large shock ring, tall mushroom cloud, ground left burning for hours); battle blasts sized by what was lost (small for infantry and drones, medium for tanks and aircraft, large for ships and buildings); smoke and fire now stay fixed to their spot on the map while zooming or panning; at most a few heavy effects at once (fewer on Low and with Battery saver), none drawn off screen; the Effects setting (High/Low) also trims them; the test build's developer panel has a button for each effect. Chemical and nuclear weapons are effects only for now (not in the game rules yet).
- Plan for 64 playable nations (no change in play yet): docs/NATIONS-PLAN.md lists the 64 nations in six regions with their capitals and 7 to 14 starting cities each, the 128 countries that stay computer-run, the rules for choosing cities, the start by strength tier, resources, victory targets and the region-by-region rollout with its tests; the numbers come from node .test/nations-analysis.js.
- Strike effects, repair: the nuclear flash now always shows (an ICBM's also whitens the top bar and side panel); cruise and ballistic blasts are bigger at normal zoom, the ballistic one plainly heavier; chemical gas is a low, pale yellow-green sheet you can see by day and at night; smoke still shows at night; shock waves are soft dust bands instead of drawn rings; the mushroom cloud is one rounded cloud that stays on screen close in; battle blasts are bigger, come every hour by the fighting counters, and are sized by what each side fields; a missile hit no longer leaves a glowing disc, and its damage number floats over the blast.

## Released
- v3.12 (commit 5b47dd8, public 2026-11-11):
  - Battery saver (setting, on by default on phones): the map redraws only when something on it changes, 30 fps cap, pauses in the background, building pictures load when first shown; repairs 1 and 2 (no redraws for convoys, storms or water off screen; names placed right after a phone rotation).
  - Battery saver: the map now runs smooth (up to 60 fps) while you drag, pinch or zoom it or the camera glides, and for a moment after; 30 fps for other animation, 10 for gentle background motion, nothing when still.
  - Research: two projects run at the same time (slot #1 and slot #2, both at full speed, for you and the AI); a project waits while its prerequisite is still running, and an old save's queued project starts in slot #2 when it loads; repair 1 (tapping a project only selects it, the Research button starts it and its slot lights up; button text no longer runs together).
  - Cities: much sharper and more detailed up close (each city keeps its outline but gets its own street plan with buildings, parks, rail yards and downtown towers; big cities are drawn in pieces at full sharpness, phones included); at night the old look is back on the new streets (lit streets and windows on dark roofs, warm glow downtown, rivers as dark water, main roads lit), every town is lit at once, and nothing pops or jumps when a zoom stops.
  - Mobilizing units: a unit can be mobilized only when it is researched and the city has its building: infantry (Motorized, Anti-Tank, Special Forces, Paratroopers) now needs a Recruiting Office in that city, vehicles an Arms Factory, aircraft an Airbase, ships a Naval Yard; a blocked unit is greyed in the Mobilize list with the reason ("Build a Recruiting Office first", "Research required: ...") and a tap says why; every nation starts with a Recruiting Office at its capital and its two biggest other cities, the AI builds more, and an old save gets one at the capital (units already in training carry on); the Mobilize Unit square now shows the unit in training with a fill that rises as it trains, and an empty dashed square when nothing trains (the gear is gone).
  - Map: all city markers are gone (the dot and ring on every city, the star-in-a-ring on capitals, the pulsing ring round your own capital, and the points of light on cities and towns at night when zoomed out); only the city names remain, in the same places; clicking or tapping a city works as before, and tapping a city's name now selects that city too.
  - New pictures: your AWACS, patrol plane, air superiority fighter, UAVs, swarm and strike drones, anti-drone truck, cargo ship, cruise and ballistic missiles and a new bold infantry picture are in the game (menus, lists, tooltips, missile menu), and the Recruiting Office and Fortifications show their own building pictures by level; the Arms Industry pictures are stored for the coming rework.
  - Campaign briefing cut down to the logo, one line, three short tips and big buttons; the rest moved to the Field Manual (Getting started), and one-line first-time tips (with Got it and Skip all tips) now teach cities, moving, battles and research.
  - Rules split into rules/*.js in 10 steps plus a repair (no change in play): the rules run on their own, even without a browser (tools/headless.js).
