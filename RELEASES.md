# Releases: dev build → public build

How it works from now on:
- Every finished change goes to the **Dev Build** first: https://claude.ai/artifact/EKDR7pckn3hPkoJVuxoe7R
- Each change is noted below under **Waiting for public**, newest last.
- The **public build** (https://claude.ai/artifact/PegnoUY4juYxMxy64YDTS4) is only updated when the owner says to move changes to public. Then everything under "Waiting for public" is published, moved to **Released**, and tagged in git.

## Currently public
- v3.12 (commit 5b47dd8): everything in v3.11, plus the battery saver (60 fps while moving the map), two research slots, sharper cities, mobilization rules, no city markers, the rules split, the new Higgsfield pictures and the short briefing.

## Waiting for public
- Strike and blast effects drawn in code (no video, light on phones), one look per weapon: cruise missile (quick orange fireball, sparks, small dark puff), ballistic missile (white flash, ground shock ring, big fireball, thick smoke column), chemical (small burst, then a yellow-green gas cloud that spreads slowly and lingers), tactical nuclear (blinding flash, fireball, fast shock ring, small mushroom cloud) and ICBM (screen-wide flash, huge fireball, large shock ring, tall mushroom cloud, ground left burning for hours); battle blasts sized by what was lost (small for infantry and drones, medium for tanks and aircraft, large for ships and buildings); smoke and fire now stay fixed to their spot on the map while zooming or panning; at most a few heavy effects at once (fewer on Low and with Battery saver), none drawn off screen; the Effects setting (High/Low) also trims them; the test build's developer panel has a button for each effect. Chemical and nuclear weapons are effects only for now (not in the game rules yet).
- Plan for 64 playable nations (no change in play yet): docs/NATIONS-PLAN.md lists the 64 nations in six regions with their capitals and 7 to 14 starting cities each, the 128 countries that stay computer-run, the rules for choosing cities, the start by strength tier, resources, victory targets and the region-by-region rollout with its tests; the numbers come from node .test/nations-analysis.js.
- Strike effects, repair: the nuclear flash now always shows (an ICBM's also whitens the top bar and side panel); cruise and ballistic blasts are bigger at normal zoom, the ballistic one plainly heavier; chemical gas is a low, pale yellow-green sheet you can see by day and at night; smoke still shows at night; shock waves are soft dust bands instead of drawn rings; the mushroom cloud is one rounded cloud that stays on screen close in; battle blasts are bigger, come every hour by the fighting counters, and are sized by what each side fields; a missile hit no longer leaves a glowing disc, and its damage number floats over the blast.
- Recruiting Office rework: it now has five levels (one picture for all). In its city every unit trains 10% faster per level (50% at level V) and the city gives +3k / +4.5k / +6k / +7.5k / +9k manpower a day; level I is still what infantry needs. Reaching each level costs $40M; $75M + 10 materials; $130M + 20 materials + 10 iron; $220M + 35 materials + 20 iron; $360M + 55 materials + 30 iron + 15 electronics, and takes 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 days (the first office was $30M). The bonus multiplies with the Arms Factory, Airbase and Naval Yard bonuses and the doctrine, but no unit ever trains in less than 30% of its base time (before, a level V factory with a top airbase or the Mass doctrine went lower). When a level finishes, units queued or in training in that city get their remaining time cut at once. The building tooltip and the Build list say what the level gives and what the next one adds, the Mobilize panel header says "Recruiting Office: 30% faster training", and the Mobilize list times include it. The computer upgrades an office only when it is short of manpower with no place left for a new one, or when a full training queue holds it back in a war. Offices in old saves are level I.
- New unit, the Stealth Bomber (STB; it uses the Heavy Bomber's picture until its own arrives): $330M, 26 oil, 34 materials, 6 iron, 40 electronics, 8 rare materials, 8k manpower, 7 days to build, needs Airbase III. It flies as far as the Heavy Bomber (6,100 km) with 60% of its bombs (3.6 damage on a city per aircraft, 1.8 elsewhere, fewer building levels wrecked, troops sheltering in a city take 25% more instead of 50%). SAMs, frigates and cruisers hit it 35% as often as a Heavy Bomber, fighters 40% as often (the Air Superiority Fighter's 3.8x bomber bonus becomes 1.5x). In flight an enemy sees it only from a Mobile SAM or jammer in or next to the province under it; an AWACS shows it as a ? contact at half its radar range and never identifies it. Two new research projects in the Air branch: Strategic Bomber Upgrade (after Strategic Bombers; Heavy Bomber range +720 km and attack +1; 8 days) and Stealth Bombers (11 days; needs both Strategic Bomber Upgrade and Stealth Airframes, and the research tree draws both lines). The computer players build it only from day 60, only at war with a power that fields SAM stacks, and keep to one wing (2 aircraft) each.
- New economy: every city now produces one main resource (money, oil, materials, iron, electronics or rare materials; shown on the city panel, the Cities list and the Resources map), spread so no country has everything and rare earth is scarce (one or two cities in some countries, none in most), so you trade or fight for what you lack. The Arms Factory is now the Army Base (ground units only, no income), the new Arms Industry raises a city's main resource by 25% per level (five levels, cheap first, dearer for each extra city), and the Refinery, Foundry and Rare Earth Mine are gone (refunded in old saves). The Economy research branch is gone too: its boosts are now buildings, and old saves keep what they researched. Incomes, research prices and the computer players' trading were rebalanced around it.
- New economy, repair 1: the Market shows every offer (the lists no longer hide most of them) and a click on a goods card shows only that good; AI nations now buy your sell offers like any other listing, so you can export; the Market, the top-bar tooltips and the defeat screen name the goods you have no city for and no longer mention removed buildings; old saves show the Economy research they keep; cities stand out on the Resources map.
- New economy together with the five-level Recruiting Office and the Stealth Bomber: the Recruiting Office speeds up every unit trained in its city (10% per level) on top of the Army Base (ground units only), Airbase and Naval Yard bonuses, never below 30% of the base time; it gives +3k to +9k manpower a day by level; computer players build Recruiting Offices and Arms Industry side by side. World income stays within 15% of the old economy's (three seeds, day 1, 10 and 30).
- Nations of the world, step 0 (plumbing; test build only): the start screen has a second world, "Nations of the world", beside "Classic" (the 15 powers on the old rules, still the one it opens on). The new world lists its nations by region (Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia, Americas, Oceania) with their rank (Superpower, Major, Regional or Minor power) and a difficulty; for now it holds today's 15 powers, and the other nations arrive region by region. In it nations start close to equal: money a day is 247 for a superpower, 233 for a major power and 220 for a regional power (in Classic China starts with about 620 and most others about 250), a big country has more land, cities and soldiers but not much more money; every nation gets border guards facing its strongest neighbour, and superpowers and major powers on a coast a Naval Yard; computer nations wait 21 days before attacking a nation two ranks below them, do not gang up on a computer nation that is already at war, and count a very large country as no easier a target than a mid-sized one. Victory numbers follow the number of nations (with 15: a quarter of the world's land or 5 capitals, as before). Classic games and every old save play exactly as before (the same save reaches the same state in the old and the new build).
- Nations of the world, step 0b (province split; test build only): in the nations world Israel, Greece, Cuba and Belarus are cut into smaller provinces with real place names (Israel 2 to 8, Greece 8 to 13, Cuba 8 to 12, Belarus 7 to 13; 21 new provinces), so each can hold 7 or 8 cities when its region opens; roads, rails, rivers, borders, labels and towns follow the new provinces. The classic world, its map and every old save are unchanged.
- Nations of the world, step 1 (Europe; test build only): Italy, Spain, Poland, Ukraine, Romania, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Greece and Belarus are now playable (25 nations, 14 in Europe), and every European nation has its planned cities, 7 to 14, named after the largest town in each (Katowice, Gdańsk, Leeds, Lille, Thessaloniki and so on): the United Kingdom, France and Germany go from 4 or 5 cities to 11, Russia from 19 to 14 (Norilsk, Anadyr, Magadan, Petropavlovsk and Khabarovsk become ordinary provinces). A superpower's or major power's starting Naval Yard no longer costs it two border guards, so major powers open with more units than regional ones. In computer-only test games Norway, Finland, Ukraine, Greece and Belarus always lasted past day 30, Russia kept its land and Germany expanded. The classic world and old saves are unchanged.
- Nations of the world, step 2 (the Middle East; test build only): Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Syria, Yemen and Israel are now playable (30 nations, 8 in the Middle East), and every Middle Eastern nation has its planned cities, 7 to 12, named after the largest town in each (Basra, Kirkuk, Erbil, Aden, Tabuk, Rasht, Bandar Abbas, Suez and so on): Turkey goes from 4 cities to 12, Iran from 5 to 12, Egypt from 3 to 11; every nation of the region has an oil city. To keep 30 computer nations quick, a computer nation at peace and away from every player now plans every 8 hours instead of every 4 (nations at war or next to a player still plan every 4). In computer-only test games Syria, Yemen and Israel always lasted past day 30, Iran kept its land and the world oil price stayed steady. The classic world and old saves are unchanged.
- Nations of the world, step 3 (Africa; test build only): Algeria, Morocco, Libya, Nigeria, Ethiopia, Sudan, DR Congo, Angola, Kenya, Tanzania, Mali, Chad, Mozambique and Cameroon are now playable (44 nations, 15 in Africa), and every African nation has its planned cities, 7 to 12, named after the largest town in each (Annaba, Constantine, Marrakesh, Fez, Port Harcourt, Mombasa, Beira, Douala and so on): South Africa goes from 3 cities to 11. Computer nations now stop grabbing independent land once they hold one and a half times their starting land, and once a computer nation has doubled its land it makes peace and only defends; two computer nations can no longer declare war on the same nation on the same day. In computer-only test games no African nation doubled its land in more than one game of five, and Mali and Chad kept a positive income. The classic world and old saves are unchanged.
- Nations of the world, step 3 repair (test build only): a nation whose cities make no oil, materials, iron or electronics now gets twice its capital's daily share of that good (Australia, Spain, Poland, Romania, Greece and Mozambique oil, Japan iron, Norway electronics), so every nation has at least 4 oil a day from day one; it still has to buy the rest. The classic world and old saves are unchanged.
- Nations of the world, step 4 (Asia; test build only): Pakistan, Bangladesh, Indonesia, South Korea, North Korea, Vietnam, Thailand, Myanmar, the Philippines, Malaysia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Afghanistan and Mongolia are now playable (58 nations, 17 in Asia), and every Asian nation has its planned cities, 7 to 14, named after the largest town in each (Nagoya, Kobe, Peshawar, Gwangju, Haiphong, Hat Yai, Zamboanga, Johor Bahru, Herat, Darkhan and so on): India goes from 7 cities to 14, Japan from 5 to 12. With so many nations the land to win is now a tenth of the world (308 provinces, more for a big country: its starting land plus 60%) or ten starting capitals. Computer nations that have doubled their land now stop their advances and leave a collapsed neighbour's land independent; after day 60 they may grow further; superpowers and major powers now go to war with each other more readily. In computer-only test games North Korea, Mongolia and Bangladesh always lasted past day 30, Japan, Indonesia and the Philippines expanded and built fleets, and long games were won on day 148 and day 215. The classic world and old saves are unchanged.
- Nations of the world, step 4 repair (test build only): the computer superpowers (China, India, Russia and the United States) now treat each other as rivals: one will attack another at a little under equal strength, even while that rival is already fighting someone else, so the giants fight each other instead of only bullying small neighbours; and a computer nation that has doubled its land stops taking more (it still retakes its own lost provinces) instead of grabbing the provinces its troops were already standing in.
