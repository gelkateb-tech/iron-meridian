# Releases: dev build → public build

How it works from now on:
- Every finished change goes to the **Dev Build** first: https://claude.ai/artifact/EKDR7pckn3hPkoJVuxoe7R
- Each change is noted below under **Waiting for public**, newest last.
- The **public build** (https://claude.ai/artifact/PegnoUY4juYxMxy64YDTS4) is only updated when the owner says to move changes to public. Then everything under "Waiting for public" is published, moved to **Released**, and tagged in git.

With no way around (a port's approach, or a chokepoint: Hormuz, Malacca, Gibraltar, Suez, Bab-el-Mandeb, Bosporus, Panama, Danish straits; one cruiser or two frigates close one) the route is cut and both partners and the blockader get a dispatch; the open ocean needs a screen of several ships. A partner can pause a route: no convoys, nothing lost, no income. The AI blockades enemy lanes at chokepoints and off enemy ports, raids, escorts raided routes and pauses routes it would lose. Old saves load unchanged.
- Fleets sail inside their own sea zone (dev build): moving a fleet to another spot in its zone is no longer instant; it sails there at its sea speed (slowest ship, research and stack penalty), drawn on the move, Halt stops it, and it fires, blockades, escorts or raids from the new spot only once it arrives (computer fleets too). Ground forces still step to a spot in their own province at once.
