# Releases: dev build → public build

How it works from now on:
- Every finished change goes to the **Dev Build** first: https://claude.ai/artifact/EKDR7pckn3hPkoJVuxoe7R
- Each change is noted below under **Waiting for public**, newest last.
- The **public build** (https://claude.ai/artifact/PegnoUY4juYxMxy64YDTS4) is only updated when the owner says to move changes to public. Then everything under "Waiting for public" is published, moved to **Released**, and tagged in git.

## Currently public
- v3.11 (commit 986e1fb): CoN layout, sleek counters, mixed-stack icon, troop models fixed while zooming, research open from day one, Arms Factory pricing, public build with no dev code.

## Waiting for public
- Battery saver (setting, on by default on phones): the map redraws only when something on it changes, 30 fps cap, pauses in the background, building pictures load when first shown; repairs 1 and 2 (no redraws for convoys, storms or water off screen; names placed right after a phone rotation).
- Battery saver: the map now runs smooth (up to 60 fps) while you drag, pinch or zoom it or the camera glides, and for a moment after; 30 fps for other animation, 10 for gentle background motion, nothing when still.
- Research: two projects run at the same time (slot #1 and slot #2, both at full speed, for you and the AI); a project waits while its prerequisite is still running, and an old save's queued project starts in slot #2 when it loads; repair 1 (tapping a project only selects it, the Research button starts it and its slot lights up; button text no longer runs together).
- Cities: much sharper and more detailed up close (each city keeps its outline but gets its own street plan with buildings, parks, rail yards and downtown towers; big cities are drawn in pieces at full sharpness, phones included); at night the old look is back on the new streets (lit streets and windows on dark roofs, warm glow downtown, rivers as dark water, main roads lit), every town is lit at once, and nothing pops or jumps when a zoom stops.
- Mobilizing units: a unit can be mobilized only when it is researched and the city has its building: infantry (Motorized, Anti-Tank, Special Forces, Paratroopers) now needs a Recruiting Office in that city, vehicles an Arms Factory, aircraft an Airbase, ships a Naval Yard; a blocked unit is greyed in the Mobilize list with the reason ("Build a Recruiting Office first", "Research required: ...") and a tap says why; every nation starts with a Recruiting Office at its capital and its two biggest other cities, the AI builds more, and an old save gets one at the capital (units already in training carry on); the Mobilize Unit square now shows the unit in training with a fill that rises as it trains, and an empty dashed square when nothing trains (the gear is gone).
- Map: all city markers are gone (the dot and ring on every city, the star-in-a-ring on capitals, the pulsing ring round your own capital, and the points of light on cities and towns at night when zoomed out); only the city names remain, in the same places; clicking or tapping a city works as before, and tapping a city's name now selects that city too.
- Rules split, step 1 of 10 (no change in play): the game script now shares one scope with the new rules files, the first one (rules/core.js) holds the event channel the rules use to report messages, sounds and effects to the screen, and the builds and tests copy and check the rules folder. Publishing now needs the files in rules/ as well.
- Rules split, step 2 of 10 (no change in play): the game's tables and constants (units, missiles, resources, terrain, buildings, research, doctrines, difficulty and victory settings), the dice, the hex-grid maths and the number-formatting helpers moved, unchanged, from index.html into rules/data.js.
- Rules split, step 3 of 10 (no change in play): the world (provinces, neighbours, straits, road and rail links, resources, terrain, population, sea lanes, seasons and weather) and the game state (scenarios, new game, save text, loading and the old-save upgrades, army and nation helpers, victory checks) moved to rules/map.js and rules/state.js; the page now draws the river, road and railway lines itself and hears about a new game, a loaded game and the end of the war through events.
- Rules split, step 4 of 10 (no change in play): the research rules (two slots, what can be researched and when it finishes) and the production rules (unit and building costs, what can be built or mobilized where, training, construction, upgrades, missile building, and the developer-mode rule overrides of the test build) moved, unchanged, from index.html into rules/research.js and rules/production.js. Publishing now also needs those two files.
- Rules split, step 5 of 10 (no change in play): the economy rules (province yields, blockade, convoys, income, the world market and order book, unrest and partisans) and the trade-route rules (ports, sea lanes, route value, raids, escorts) moved, unchanged, from index.html into rules/economy.js and rules/trade.js; the convoy-sunk explosion is now drawn by the screen when the rules report a raid.
- Rules split, step 6 of 10 (no change in play): the movement rules (unit speeds, roads and railways, route finding, move orders, splitting and merging armies, supply and upkeep) and the diplomacy rules (relations, opinion, wars and surprise attacks, peace deals, coalitions, messages, trades, gifts, coalition aid and espionage) now live in rules/movement.js and rules/diplomacy.js; the war warning popup, message toasts and the counter's start mark are now things the page does when the rules report them.
- Rules split, step 7 of 10 (no change in play): the combat rules (frontage and flanking, commanders, digging in, artillery fire, joining and chasing forces, losses, capturing provinces, stances, battle odds) and the fog-of-war rules (what you can see, hidden submarines and special forces, early-warning contacts) moved, unchanged, to rules/combat.js and rules/vision.js; the shell and capture effects, the flanking achievement and the selection follow-up after a merge are now events the screen listens for.
