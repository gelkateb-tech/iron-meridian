Generated by `node tools/resources-list.js` from `tools/build-nations.js` (table `MAINS`) and the rules. Do not edit: change the table, run
`node tools/build-nations.js`, then this tool.

The owner's resource balance rule (`docs/ECONOMY.md`, "Resource balance"): every playable nation keeps its 7 to 14 cities; each of the six
goods (money, oil, materials, iron, electronics, rare materials) is the main resource of at least one of its cities; no good is the main
resource of more than 2 of its cities, except that a nation of 13 or 14 cities gives its 1 or 2 cities beyond 12 the good that fits its
region best, so that good reaches 3 (a 14-city nation has two goods on 3). Resources follow the real geography where the map allows.
