# Research screen reference (Conflict of Nations)

The owner's notes on Conflict of Nations' research screen, kept as the reference for the research redesign.

**How to use this file**
- Don't build from it yet.
- Use it for structure and feel only. All icons, art and names in Iron Meridian must be our own; nothing is copied from Conflict of Nations.

## Layout
- Top: 2 research slots side by side (we already have 2).
- Main menu: one wide banner per category, with a diamond icon on the left, the name plus a one-line role, and a photo behind it.
- Inside a category: one column per unit line, with a day scale down the left (1, 2, 3 … 20). Each node sits on the day it becomes available.
- Nodes are diamonds joined by lines (prerequisites). Some lines branch sideways, where one unit leads to a related one.
- Node colours: blue = researched, green = can research now, grey = locked.
- Some nodes have a small number badge (2–4), probably how many levels that node has.
- Elite versions of a unit have a star-and-wings badge.

## Node types
1. Unit unlock: the unit's picture on green, at the top of its column.
2. Next generation: the same unit with a new picture (newer model), further down (around days 8–17).
3. Stat upgrades in between, each with a simple white icon: health, damage, speed, vision, range, terrain handling, chemical/nuclear protection, air-transportable, carrier-capable, cheaper/faster to build, more missiles.

## Categories (role, then units with their first day)
- Infantry, conquer & defend territory: motorized (1), mechanized (1), airmobile (1), a light vehicle unit (4), special forces (1). 2nd generations on days 12–17.
- Armored, heavy damage supporting infantry: APC (1), IFV (1), amphibious vehicle (2), main battle tank (1), tank destroyer (1).
- Support, anti-air, artillery & radar: towed artillery (1), self-propelled artillery (3), rocket artillery (5), anti-air gun vehicle (1), mobile SAM (2). Mostly range upgrades.
- Helicopters, short air range: light heli (1), attack heli (2), naval heli (2), elite heli (1).
- Fighters, medium air range: several fighter lines from day 1, with variants branching off at days 4–5 and 9; a UAV (1); an elite fighter (2) that becomes carrier-capable on day 7.
- Heavies, long air range: heavy bomber (3), maritime patrol (4), AWACS (5).
- Naval, surface sea control: patrol boat (1), frigate (1), destroyer (2), cruiser (2), a larger warship (3).
- Submarines, stealth: attack sub (1), ballistic missile sub (5), elite sub (1). Subs can launch missiles: attack subs fire sub-launched cruise missiles, ballistic missile subs fire sub-launched ballistic missiles.
- Missiles, launched from other units: warhead chain conventional (1) → chemical (3) → nuclear (5) → ballistic missile (6); cruise missile (2) with range and speed upgrades; sub-launched cruise and ballistic missiles; ICBMs (our addition) as the longest-range top tier, launched from Missile Silos.
- Officers, unique strong units that boost armies: one per branch (ground, air, naval, mechanized, armor), each with damage and health upgrades.
- Seasons: seasonal special units.
- Loadout, temporary performance boosts: 4 ammo types with 2–3 levels each.
