START-ARMIES: the two outer starting stacks of every nation (branch start-armies, rules/state.js startOuter; the same in every seed, mode and seat)
Rule: the first stack goes to the biggest city outside the capital, the second to the biggest city away from the first: on another side of the capital (at least 60 degrees
apart as seen from it) or far from the first (at least 3/4 of the capital-to-first distance from both the first and the capital). A city on another nation's land border
(independent land does not count) or on a highway counts as 50% bigger; ties go to the more populous city. Only cities the capital reaches over its own land count (no exclaves or islands),
never a city next to the capital or next to the other stack. Fallbacks: cities next to the capital, a second city on the same side, any other city, border provinces, the capital.
Each stack: 2 infantry now; + 1 recon vehicle once the owner approves it (START_ARMY.recon in rules/data.js). #n = size rank among the nation's other cities; [next to: ...] = the foreign land the city touches (only another nation counts for the weight).
